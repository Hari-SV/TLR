Camera & Gesture Recognition Notice

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Why does the game need camera access?

Shipped Studio's game uses your device's camera to provide hands-free gameplay.

The camera allows our system to detect hand gestures, including American Sign Language (ASL) and other hand signs, and convert those gestures into gameplay input.

What happens to my camera feed?

When camera gameplay is enabled, camera input is transmitted to our cloud-based processing infrastructure, where a machine-learning model analyzes the input and produces gameplay commands.

Is my video stored?

No.

Shipped Studio does not intentionally store or retain your webcam photographs or video.

Camera input is processed for the purpose of providing gameplay functionality and is not retained after the processing necessary to recognize the gameplay input.

Does the game identify me?

No.

The camera system is not used for facial recognition or personal identification.

We do not use the camera to determine who you are.

Does the game create a biometric profile?

No.

The camera system is designed to recognize gameplay gestures rather than identify individual users.

We do not create biometric identification profiles from your camera input.

Can I disable the camera?

Yes.

You can disable camera functionality at any time using the designated in-game hotkey.

You can also manage camera permissions through your browser or operating system.

Disabling the camera will prevent hands-free gesture-based gameplay from functioning.

Questions

For questions concerning camera processing or privacy, please contact Shipped Studio through the contact information provided on our website.
